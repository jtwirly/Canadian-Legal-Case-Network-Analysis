# SCC 1877-2026 Future Law Detector

https://claude.ai/public/artifacts/744ec8fe-f57b-4441-8fba-df5e15645ebe

Built from 10,867 SCC decisions. Counts come from full-text keyword scans checked for false positives (citation-list mentions, idiomatic hits like "current climate" got filtered out).

What it surfaced:
- **AI-generated evidence** — only 1 mention in the entire 149-year corpus (2025), and it's a passing aside, not a holding. Genuinely unlitigated.
- **Tech-enabled child exploitation sentencing** — 46 cases since 2010, accelerating hard, with real doctrinal shifts (*Friesen* 2020, *Bertrand Marchand* 2023).
- **Platform jurisdiction** — only 3 cases, each pulling a different direction (*Equustek*, *Douez v. Facebook*, *Uber v. Heller*).
- **IP addresses as private info** — a 12-year build-up resolved 5–4 in *Bykovets* (2024), included as a contrast case for what "just settled" looks like.
- **Federal climate policy vs. provincial power** — resolved through two references (2021, 2023).

Caveat: this corpus is SCC judgments only — no lower courts, legislation, regulatory decisions, or academic literature, so something can be very live everywhere else and still look thin here just because it hasn't reached the top court yet.
